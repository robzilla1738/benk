# ADR-014 — Two product tracks and a narrow initial market

**Status:** Accepted — product sequencing
**Date:** 2026-10-08
**Owner:** founding technical/product lead

## Context
The product must combine a fast native collaboration experience with inspectable, bounded local/cloud execution. This decision narrows implementation ambiguity while preserving the user's fixed requirements. Read the linked domain/security specifications before treating a mechanism as complete.

## Decision
Maintain communication replacement breadth and agent-enabled outcome quality as distinct tracks. Initial design partners are cross-functional software/product teams, with a complete checkout-issue workflow as the first vertical slice. Personal utility and a browser reviewer allow project-level adoption before workspace migration.

## Consequences and alternatives
An ever-larger bot or integration list is not success. Keep agents quiet by default and prioritize decision-needed updates. Avoid forcing a new IDE, marketplace, swarm system, general browser, or proprietary version control into the first release. Broader enterprise capabilities are requirements-driven expansion, not forgotten obligations.

## Validation and revisit trigger
Gate: repeated independent team use, measured accepted outcomes and human review effort, willingness to pay, trustworthy execution, and elimination of concrete switching blockers. The parity register is a candidate inventory requiring current verification, not proof of 1:1 Slack equivalence. Industry leadership is an evidence-backed aspiration.
